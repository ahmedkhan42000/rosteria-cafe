Our Story

Where the coffee's hot and the hours are slow

Since 2026

Where coffee meets chai

Rösteria Café started with a simple idea: bring specialty coffee and house-shaken matcha together with the flavors of karak chai and crispy onion pakora — and give every table a board game so nobody's in a rush. Today the espresso machine runs all day, the pastry case never sits empty, and the chessboard by the window rarely stays free for long.
